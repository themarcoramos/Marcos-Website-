The Synthesis

I operate at the intersection of public policy, operational strategy, and applied technology. My work is anchored in "Dignity in Infrastructure" and the conviction that the administrative and digital systems must reflect and protect the human worth of the communities they serve. Rather than viewing law and code as separate disciplines, I use both as practical instruments to translate statutory complexity into tangible, equitable public operations.

The Journey: The Capital & The Code

Raised in the Central Valley (Ivanhoe, California) as a first-generation student, my perspective was shaped by learning to navigate rooms where underestimation was the baseline. That background became my greatest analytical advantage. Today, I bridge the divide between legislative intent and technical execution: leveraging Python (Pandas/NumPy) to model reserve health across nine-figure public budgets ($103M fiscal audit), managing enterprise IT infrastructure, and engineering digital advocacy pipelines that mobilize stakeholders across all 58 California counties.

The Pursuit: Applied Governance & Scale

My focus centers on long-term institutional modernization. As an aspiring public administration and political science lecturer, I aim to bring active Capitol complexities and quantitative methods into the classroom. In practice, that means architecting the legal research, data workflows, and administrative infrastructure that make equitable outcomes sustainable; from judicial bottleneck mitigation across 5,000+ litigant caseloads to coalition campaigns that protected $60M in statewide behavioral health funding.
