Marco A. Ramos Jr.

Policy & Government Relations Professional | MPA

What I Do

I’m a Sacramento-based policy professional with a Master of Public Administration. I analyze legislation, testify on it, and build the data tools and software that put policy into practice. I believe the legal and digital systems people depend on should protect their dignity, and my work increasingly focuses on AI governance and data protection in youth- and community-serving systems.

Background

I grew up in the Central Valley (Ivanhoe, California) as a first-generation student, and my perspective was shaped by learning to navigate rooms where underestimation was the baseline. I earned my B.A. in political science (magna cum laude) at California State University, Fresno, and my MPA at California State University, Long Beach. Before CAMHPRO, I worked as a legal assistant in a California superior court, where I conducted statutory research and audited case-flow operations across a 5,000+ litigant caseload.

Current Work

At CAMHPRO, I analyze California bills, oppose expansions of involuntary commitment through testimony and coalition advocacy, and run digital advocacy: weekly email campaigns, plain-language bill summaries and alerts, and an automated letter tool that has sent 1,500+ messages on 17 bills. At Big Brothers Big Sisters of the Greater Sacramento Area, I designed and built the enrollment and compliance-tracking platform the organization now uses, and I serve as its lead IT administrator. For an MPA project, I built a Python (Pandas/NumPy) fiscal audit engine and applied it to the public Form 990 filings of the American Bar Association Fund for Justice and Education. I’m bilingual in English and Spanish.

Outside Work

I play trombone in a community symphony, do bodybuilding, and skate distance on ice and roller skates. I’m also a top 1% Rainbow Six Siege player.
