Marco A. Ramos Jr.

Policy & Government Relations Professional | MPA

I grew up in Ivanhoe, California, a small farm town in the Central Valley, and I was a first-generation student. In a way I always will be, because I consider myself a lifelong learner.

Growing up, I watched peers fall into gangs, violence, and drugs once the school day ended. Looking back, it was a town with little support outside the classroom, and that experience gave me a clear compass: no amount of underestimation will limit what we are all capable of. That lesson follows me in everything I do. Whether it’s a piece of state legislation, a court docket, or an internal database, public systems should always protect human dignity.

Today I work in Sacramento, where law, data, and people meet. I analyze California bills, testify before legislative committees, and build the software and data tools that turn policy into something people can use.

Outside Work

I play trombone in a community symphony. Playing low brass taught me deep listening, dynamic balance, and knowing when to step forward and when to support the ensemble. I also skate distance on roller and ice skates, lift in the gym, and play Rainbow Six Siege in the top 1% of players. I try to approach my work the way I approach a piece of music or a high-stakes match: with steady focus and respect for the people in the room.
