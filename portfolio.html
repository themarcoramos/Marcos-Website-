Urban Policy & Educational Equity

MPA project (PPA 610): analysis of California's Local Control Funding Formula (LCFF), created through AB 97 and SB 91 to direct more funding to low-income students, English learners, and foster youth. Covers the 2025–26 and 2026–27 state budgets, the debate over attendance-based versus enrollment-based funding, the Equity Multiplier, and the Community Schools model, and ends with five recommendations. Budget figures updated October 2026.

Multi-Jurisdictional Policy Analysis

MPA project (PPA 670): compares three ways to share Colorado River shortages (no action, seniority-based cuts, and uniform percentage cuts) across seven states, 30 Tribes, and Mexico, using the Bardach & Patashnik eightfold path. Recommends uniform reductions with voluntary, compensated Tribal participation. Updated October 2026 for the federal Record of Decision.
